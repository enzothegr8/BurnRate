Rules

The segment order never changes. Solid, dashed, dotted, left to right, is the confidence notation in order. It is not a decorative rhythm and it may not be rearranged for balance.

The wordmark carries the color; the rule does not. All three segments are jet, so the marks are distinguished by line style alone.

On jet the lockup is monochrome. Page white throughout, including the tagline and the period. The domain colors are not lifted to survive the surface, because a lifted color is a new color and the palette is closed. Same class of decision as the jet-only favicon.

The masthead is md. The three named sizes are the three sizes. A bespoke size in a component is a fourth one nobody approved.

Below the small size shown above, drop the tagline rather than shrinking it. The wordmark and rule can go smaller. The line cannot.

The tagline belongs to the mark, not to the writing. It does not appear in body copy, headlines, or social captions as a phrase.

Two colors only in the monogram. The overlap value is a product, not a choice, and must never be set by hand or substituted.

One thing to decide before launch

The favicon. Look at the 16px renders above. At that size the multiply overlap fills in and the two letters begin to read as a single shape. The jet-only version beside it holds together better because there is no seam color to muddy.

Shipping the two-color monogram at 32px and above and a jet-only version at 16px is the normal solution and the one I would take. It is a technical fallback rather than a second logo, so it does not need its own approval every time.
